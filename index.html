How it works
1
Export from Ringba — Go to Call Logs, set your date range, export as CSV with the Recording column included.
2
Upload the CSV — Drop it in Setup. The app filters to sub-1-minute calls only — longer calls are ignored.
3
Whisper transcription — Each recording is downloaded and sent to OpenAI Whisper which converts the audio to text.
4
Claude classification — Claude reads the actual transcript and classifies the call based on what was really said.
5
Export results — Download as CSV or push directly to a Google Sheet including the full transcripts.
